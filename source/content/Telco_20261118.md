### 1. Scanning probe microscopy (SPM) application definitions

- **RM:** Presented the changes to [nexusformat/definitions#1632](https://github.com/nexusformat/definitions/pull/1632) following the Hamburg review. The scanner frame is the root reference frame; the scan, probe, stage, and sample frames describe the geometry where available. The stage frame accommodates Bruker files that distinguish the stage from the scanner. Sample and probe geometry is not always available from the instrument files.
- **LP:** Explained that the optional `NXcoordinate_system` information lets users describe how components relate without requiring metadata they do not have. Offered converted example datasets for reviewers and noted that project partners already use the definitions in their labs.
- **RM:** Reported that the converter handles five instrument file variants. After manually checking hundreds of datasets, the team is building a pipeline to convert public SPM data from Zenodo to NeXus for NOMAD.
- **AB:** Asked reviewers to revisit and resolve the outstanding comments they had raised. Proposed that a NIAC member finish the review and, if the PR is ready, put it to an online vote before merging it into the main application definitions.
- **SB:** Suggested inviting an SPM specialist to answer questions and participate as a community reviewer. **LP** noted that project specialists could help answer questions but would be reviewing work they had helped develop. **FD** said an independent opinion would be valuable for a standard application definition, and **AB** argued that NIAC should now complete its own review.
- **FD:** Volunteered to review the changes against the original comments and resolve those that had been addressed. A second reviewer would be welcome. No vote was taken.

### 2. NXDL technical meetings and group validity

- **WD:** Summarized [nexusformat/definitions#1687](https://github.com/nexusformat/definitions/issues/1687). Proposed distinguishing properties (what an item is, such as a group's name or NeXus class) from attributes (what it has). HDF5 stores the class as `NX_class`, but that storage detail does not make it an ordinary attribute in the data model.
- **WD:** Presented a questionnaire, linked from Zulip, on how to handle group names and classes, including missing values and special cases at the root. The technical group covered the first three questions at its most recent meeting. The goal is to reach consensus in the technical meetings and submit a documentation PR; whether to encode the rules in NXDL is a separate question.
- **WD:** Distinguished *coverage* (whether NeXus rules apply) from *validity* (whether covered content meets them). Presented a preliminary assessment of the impact on readers, writers, and validators, for tool maintainers to check. Emphasized that compatibility with existing NeXus files should carry more weight than the cost of changing tools.
- **AB:** Suggested that validators ignore non-root groups without `NX_class`, since authors may intentionally include non-NeXus content. **WD** presented allowing such groups with a warning as another option. No rule was adopted.
- **WD:** Will send a poll for the next NXDL technical meeting and invited participants to add their answers to the shared questionnaire. **FD** asked that the technical group continue to report progress to NIAC.
