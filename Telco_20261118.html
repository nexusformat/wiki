Telco 20261118¶

Date¶

Wednesday, 18th Nov, 15:00 UTC

Connect¶

  • Zoom videoconference

Agenda¶

  • Scanning probe microscopy (SPM): follow-up from the Hamburg face-to-face meeting

  • NXDL technical meetings: group names, classes, and validation rules

  • Validation working group

  • Crystallographic base classes for neutron time-of-flight powder diffraction

  • Pull request review and future agendas

Present¶

  • NIAC: FD (Fabio Dall’Antonia), RB (Russ Berg), AB (Aaron Brewster), SB (Sandor Brockhauser), WD (Wout de Nolf), RO (Raymond Osborn), BW (Benjamin Watts), PC (Peter Chang), AB (Aaron Brewster), HG (Heike Görzig), IL(Iryana Lypova), BW (Ben Watts), SB (Sandor Brockhauser), RO (Ray Osborn), WdN (Wout de Nolf)

  • Other: LP (Lukas Pielsticker), RM (Rubel Mozumder), SeB (Seb Busch)

Minutes¶

1. Scanning probe microscopy (SPM) application definitions¶

  • RM: Presented the changes to nexusformat/definitions#1632 following the Hamburg review. The scanner frame is the root reference frame; the scan, probe, stage, and sample frames describe the geometry where available. The stage frame accommodates Bruker files that distinguish the stage from the scanner. Sample and probe geometry is not always available from the instrument files.

  • LP: Explained that the optional NXcoordinate_system information lets users describe how components relate without requiring metadata they do not have. Offered converted example datasets for reviewers and noted that project partners already use the definitions in their labs.

  • RM: Reported that the converter handles five instrument file variants. After manually checking hundreds of datasets, the team is building a pipeline to convert public SPM data from Zenodo to NeXus for NOMAD.

  • AB: Asked reviewers to revisit and resolve the outstanding comments they had raised. Proposed that a NIAC member finish the review and, if the PR is ready, put it to an online vote before merging it into the main application definitions.

  • SB: Suggested inviting an SPM specialist to answer questions and participate as a community reviewer. LP noted that project specialists could help answer questions but would be reviewing work they had helped develop. FD said an independent opinion would be valuable for a standard application definition, and AB argued that NIAC should now complete its own review.

  • FD: Volunteered to review the changes against the original comments and resolve those that had been addressed. A second reviewer would be welcome. No vote was taken.

2. NXDL technical meetings and group validity¶

  • WD: Summarized nexusformat/definitions#1687. Proposed distinguishing properties (what an item is, such as a group’s name or NeXus class) from attributes (what it has). HDF5 stores the class as NX_class, but that storage detail does not make it an ordinary attribute in the data model.

  • WD: Presented a questionnaire, linked from Zulip, on how to handle group names and classes, including missing values and special cases at the root. The technical group covered the first three questions at its most recent meeting. The goal is to reach consensus in the technical meetings and submit a documentation PR; whether to encode the rules in NXDL is a separate question.

  • WD: Distinguished coverage (whether NeXus rules apply) from validity (whether covered content meets them). Presented a preliminary assessment of the impact on readers, writers, and validators, for tool maintainers to check. Emphasized that compatibility with existing NeXus files should carry more weight than the cost of changing tools.

  • AB: Suggested that validators ignore non-root groups without NX_class, since authors may intentionally include non-NeXus content. WD presented allowing such groups with a warning as another option. No rule was adopted.

  • WD: Will send a poll for the next NXDL technical meeting and invited participants to add their answers to the shared questionnaire. FD asked that the technical group continue to report progress to NIAC.

3. Validation working group¶

  • FD: Suggested coordinating validator development with the NXDL work and raised nxvalidate as a possible tool to focus on. Noted that a validation channel is available in Zulip.

  • LP: Recommended deferring alignment between validators until the NXDL discussions are further along.

  • RB: Relayed that RO was unable to speak because of a concurrent meeting. There was no further working-group discussion or decision.

4. Crystallographic base classes¶

  • FD: Flagged nexusformat/definitions#1677 and the related nexusformat/definitions#1678, prompted by YM’s work on neutron time-of-flight powder diffraction. The PR proposes using NXphase and NXunit_cell in NXsample and NXsample_component and deprecating overlapping flat fields.

  • FD: Noted earlier input from LP and PJ and invited further comments on the PR. FD may invite YM to a future call.

5. Community updates and future agendas¶

  • LP: Reported plans to expand work on X-ray diffraction (XRD) beyond the existing contributed definition and invited interested collaborators.

  • FD: Deferred the reflectivity item until the initiative and the Open Reflectometry Standards Organisation (ORSO) align on a proposal.

  • FD: Invited members to flag items that need decisions and noted that votes can also take place between meetings. WD suggested selecting a small number of PRs for decisions. AB described starting with the most recent or active PRs when no items had been submitted in advance. FD encouraged contributors to flag older PRs that need attention.
